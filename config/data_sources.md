- **Source watersheds:** 

Oregon: [Surface Water Drinking Areas](https://geohub.oregon.gov/maps/oregon-geo::surface-water-drinking-water-source-areas) dataset, maintained by the State of Oregon on GeoHub data. Downloaded on 6/25/26. 

Washington: [Surface Water Protection Areas](https://geo.wa.gov/datasets/WADOH::source-water-protection-areas-1/about) dataset, maintained by the State of Washington. A limited version of the dataset is publicly available at the link, but the version in EMBER is the full dataset (not limited to 30 miles upstream of the catchment area), which was received upon request from the Washington Department of Health. Thanks Washington DOH! Downloaded on 7/14/26.

- **Wildfire burn perimeters and burn severity:** [MTBS CONUS](https://burnseverity.cr.usgs.gov/direct-download) dataset.
