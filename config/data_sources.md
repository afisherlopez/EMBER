- **Source watersheds (OR and WA only), outlined in blue:**

Oregon: [Surface Water Drinking Areas](https://geohub.oregon.gov/maps/oregon-geo::surface-water-drinking-water-source-areas) dataset, maintained by the State of Oregon on GeoHub data. Downloaded on 6/25/26.

Washington: [Surface Water Protection Areas](https://geo.wa.gov/datasets/WADOH::source-water-protection-areas-1/about) dataset, maintained by the State of Washington. A limited version of the dataset is publicly available at the link, but the version in EMBER is the full dataset (not limited to 30 miles upstream of the catchment area), which was received upon request from the Washington Department of Health. Thanks Washington DOH! Downloaded on 7/14/26.

- **Utility service areas (CA only), outlined in green:**

[California Water District Boundaries](https://data.cnra.ca.gov/dataset/i03-waterdistricts) gives the boundaries for "all public water agencies in California including public water systems, agricultural water districts, urban water districts, Federal and State water contractors, wholesalers, retailers, and other public or private utilities that deliver water to the end user". Downloaded on 8/5/26.

- **Utility-source connections (CA only):**

[Surface water sources, sales, and transfers for California community water systems](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.z08kprrw2). EMBER follows direct and wholesale water-system connections in the dataset to list display surface-water sources. Approximate source access points from the original project's [ArcGIS dashboard](https://bit.ly/CA_watersources_network) are drawn in blue. The original project randomly perturbed those points by up to roughly 800 meters for presentation.

- **Wildfire burn perimeters and burn severity:** [MTBS CONUS](https://burnseverity.cr.usgs.gov/direct-download) dataset.
